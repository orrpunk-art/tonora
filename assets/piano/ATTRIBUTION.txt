Tonora bundles 17 unmodified FLAC recordings (velocity layer 8), A2–A6
at minor-third intervals. This is a compact subset, not the full instrument.
Tonora decodes the first eight seconds, removes leading recording pre-roll,
interpolates neighboring pitches, and applies note release, mixing and optional
reverb/delay at playback. It does not implement the original SFZ mapping,
velocity switching, release samples or pedal-resonance system.
